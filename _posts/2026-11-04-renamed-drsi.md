The programme was called NS-RSI-Anything (neuro-symbolic recursive self-improvement). It is now
**DRSI-Anything: Delegative Recursive Self-Improvement**.

The common thread of every run so far is delegation. The LLM improvers never act in a game, a robot scene or a
desktop themselves. They write a delegate, a program that acts, evaluate it, and improve it generation after
generation; everything the loop learns is stored in that delegate. A neuro-symbolic program, readable code and
notes, is the delegate we use most, and it can itself call smaller models as tools, as in the OSWorld runs that
give the program a local vision-language model. But it is one form of delegation among several, and the new name
leaves room for the others.

Nothing else changes: the experiments, seeds and results reported earlier stay as they are, and the site keeps
its address.
