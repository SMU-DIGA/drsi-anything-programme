Deep RL updates weights. Prompt optimization tunes instructions. We let LLMs rewrite the program itself, keep a version only if it wins on seeds it never saw, and repeat.

TL;DR

An LLM writes a program that solves a task, such as an Atari bot, a robot-arm controller or a desktop agent. A population of LLM improvers (Codex CLI, Claude Code, DeepSeek) then improves that program over many generations, using only feedback from interaction with the environment. Promotion is gated on hidden seeds, and we evaluate on the game, embodied-robotics and computer-use benchmarks the community reports on most often.

Abstract

Deep reinforcement learning improves agents by adjusting weights. Recent evidence suggests that coding agents editing hand-written programs can reach competitive scores on Atari, MuJoCo and VizDoom without any gradient step, and that cross-domain recursive self-improvement loops benefit from frozen evaluation protocols. NS-RSI-Anything combines the two into a neuro-symbolic loop: neural models (LLMs) do the reasoning and the editing, while what is learned is stored symbolically, as readable, testable code and notes that persist across generations. A program, consisting of a policy, its skills and its notes, is improved generation after generation by a heterogeneous population of LLM improvers. Each candidate is re-evaluated in an isolated process and promoted only by a paired test on hidden seeds. We study when such loops keep improving, what transfers across tasks, and how they compare with RL under the same environment-step budget, on games, embodied manipulation (LIBERO, ManiSkill3, SimplerEnv, RoboTwin 2.0) and computer use (OSWorld).

Design

Improve the program, not the weights.

A program is the whole "heuristic system": the policy, its state detectors and skills, and notes holding environment facts, failed ideas and a dated changelog. Each generation runs four steps.

1

Propose

The incumbent is copied into one isolated workspace per improver, with a task brief, per-seed feedback, the full history of earlier attempts, and an evaluator restricted to the train seeds.

2

Re-evaluate

Candidates are re-scored independently. The environment runs in one process and the policy in another, so a policy never sees the reward, the seed or the environment object.

3

Promote

The best candidates face the incumbent on hidden dev seeds under a paired bootstrap gate. Test seeds are used once, at the end.

4

Simplify

The winner is simplified and kept only if its dev score does not drop. This counters the build-up of patches.

Results

Results are added as experiments finish. Rows marked are the promoted version. Grey rows are references.

What the program learned by itself

Its notes record that the ball must be served with FIRE, that frame skip calls for a paddle dead-band, and which RAM bytes hold the paddle and ball positions, confirmed by measurement rather than assumed.

Full runs with all three improvers, more seeds and more generations are underway.

No results yet

LIBERO and ManiSkill3 run headless in our setup. Task adapters with state and vision modes are in progress. MetaWorld and MuJoCo locomotion already run through the same loop.

Planned

OSWorld comes after the embodied track, with parallel VMs and tasks split by application.
